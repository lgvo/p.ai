Design documents are authoritative for their named subject.

Before substantial design work or a consequential documentation decision,
read `../PROJECT.md` and route the subject through its authority map.

- Keep each normative rule in one design document.
- README summarizes the product and links to design documents.
- FAQ explains decisions and tradeoffs; it does not define contracts.
- Design-space research describes external projects and positioning; it is not normative.
- Temporary review documents record pending decisions and are deleted after incorporation.
